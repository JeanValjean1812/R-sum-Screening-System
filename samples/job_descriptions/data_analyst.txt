Data Analyst

We are looking for a Data Analyst to help our teams make better decisions with
data. You will build dashboards, run analyses and communicate insights to
non-technical stakeholders.

Required Skills:
- SQL
- Excel
- Power BI
- Statistics

Preferred Skills:
- Python
- Tableau
- Pandas
- ETL

Experience:
- 2+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence
- Experience building dashboards and reports

Responsibilities:
- Collect, clean and analyse data from multiple sources
- Build and maintain dashboards and visualisations
- Present findings and recommendations to stakeholders
